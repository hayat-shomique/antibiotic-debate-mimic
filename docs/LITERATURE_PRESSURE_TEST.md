# Literature pressure test

Scope: claims C1-C11 from the two-agent antibiotic debate study (Qwen3-4B-instruct, 4-bit, temperature 0, fixed seed, 200 MIMIC-IV bloodstream-infection cases, 400 ordering-runs, closed 17-drug formulary, scored against each patient's laboratory susceptibility panel).

Compiled 19 August 2026 from four adversarial search-and-referee passes (sycophancy-clinical, multiagent-medical, antibiotic-llm, order-and-position, metric-degeneracy). Every referee returned survives=false on the cluster it was assigned. That verdict is carried through below without softening.

Reading rule for this document: MEASURED means the paper ran the experiment and reports the number; ARGUED means the paper asserts the position without measuring it. Identifiers are only used where a verifier resolved them. Section 5 lists the ones that could not be resolved and must not be cited.

## 2. The novelty statement

### What is genuinely new (five sentences)

1. The conjunction is new: a near-constant opening policy, near-universal capitulation under a single counterpart turn, and a net-negative before/after transition matrix, all measured on the same 400 ordering-runs against the same per-patient laboratory arbiter, Reitich (arXiv:2604.18245) supplies the two-rate decomposition with no clinical arbiter, and Antonie et al. (DOI 10.3390/antibiotics15040368) supply susceptibility arbitration of an LLM antibiotic recommendation with no revision step.
2. Repair-and-hold rates conditioned on the drug the run entered on (C9) are not reported by the panel-in-prompt items found: De Vito et al. (DOI 10.1016/j.cmi.2025.03.002) and the BCID2 chatbot study (PMC12247004) report accuracy given the laboratory result, not transitions conditioned on the entering choice.
3. Susceptibility-panel arbitration inside a two-agent clinical debate is unoccupied in this search: Antonie et al. is single-pass, SafeRx-Agent (arXiv:2605.29146) is multi-agent on MIMIC but arbitrated by the prescriptions actually given, and Iapascurta et al. (DOI 10.4258/hir.2025.31.2.209) run one MIMIC-IV vignette judged by two intensivists.
4. Holding the prompt fixed across two open checkpoints and getting two different constants localises which drug is emitted to the weights, a control that MedPIC-Bench (arXiv:2608.03028) and the causal-sensitivity study (arXiv:2605.30590) do not run, since they measure graded insensitivity within models rather than replicating constancy across checkpoints.
5. The three-cell partition carrying an explicit indeterminate class (350 + 24 + 26 = 400) extends a two-rate literature that is binary in before-and-after correctness throughout (arXiv:2604.18245, arXiv:2606.01637, arXiv:2502.08177, arXiv:2606.19826).

### What is NOT novel, said plainly

- **Position abandonment under peer challenge.** Published repeatedly, including for 4B-class open-weight models in medicine. The 44-98% range is the literature figure quoted by arXiv:2605.12991; 400/400 is the ceiling of a distribution others already mapped, and at n=400 on one checkpoint it is weaker evidence than a rate measured across 9-16 models.
- **Correctness-blind peer adoption.** The core result of BenchForm (arXiv:2501.13381), quantified bidirectionally in arXiv:2606.01637, classified flip-by-flip in arXiv:2606.00820.
- **The three-rung pressure ladder.** arXiv:2606.00820 runs it as parallel counterfactuals on identical agent-question pairs and adds a form-versus-content rung this study does not have.
- **The deference-clause ablation.** arXiv:2607.27304 ran it in medicine one month earlier with a measured noise floor; arXiv:2605.12991 ran the harder base-versus-Instruct version.
- **Harmful revision and beneficial correction as metrics.** Named in arXiv:2606.01637, formalised as corruption and correction rates in arXiv:2604.18245, and reported as regressive and progressive sycophancy in arXiv:2502.08177.
- **Speaking-order sensitivity and the paired both-orders protocol.** Standard since arXiv:2305.17926 and arXiv:2306.05685 for judges; applied to debate agents in arXiv:2602.06526, arXiv:2506.11825 and arXiv:2510.10002; a survey (arXiv:2607.26212) already names its neglect as a field-level gap.
- **Multi-agent systems failing to beat a single-agent baseline.** Kim et al. 2026, arXiv:2505.12371, arXiv:2601.12307, arXiv:2603.06856, arXiv:2606.13003, arXiv:2602.01011.
- **Coverage against susceptibility being maximised by a constant broad-spectrum policy.** Stated in print by Corbin et al. 2022; measured on MIMIC-IV gram-negative BSI by Liu et al. (rs-9854692), where meropenem-for-all at 93.2% beat their fitted model at 91.1%.
- **Susceptibility panel as arbiter for an LLM antibiotic recommendation.** Antonie et al., April 2026, 158-admission microbiology-evaluable paired subset, matched OR 2.24 (95% CI 1.08-4.63), exact McNemar p = 0.0351.
- **Clinical LLMs asserting content never supplied, with denominators.** 7.31-19.44% of 12,197 outputs (BMJ HCI 2026); 1.47% of 12,999 sentences (npj Digit Med 2025); 97.2% of scenarios before filtering (npj Digit Med 2026). 13.8% is a replication inside that band.
- **Degenerate one-class collapse of LLM and debate pipelines on MIMIC-IV-derived data.** arXiv:2604.01113.

### Lead with this

"We replicate a documented failure mode, position abandonment under peer challenge, with adoption blind to correctness, under a different arbiter, and we run the degenerate-policy comparator alongside it. The recommendation is scored against the patient's own laboratory susceptibility panel rather than a benchmark key, an LLM judge or a human preference label. The contribution is measurement validity, not the discovery of sycophancy: the finding we defend as new is the joint one, that abandonment sits on top of a near-constant opening policy, so what is abandoned carried no patient-specific information in the first place."

## 3. The attacks to prepare for

### Attack 1. MED-STRESS puts Qwen3-4B at belief-stability 80.41. Your 400/400 is a harness artefact.

The published record places this exact checkpoint size in the robust band, not the fragile one, arXiv:2605.23932 reports Qwen3-4B at IDC 82.00, BSP 80.41, knowledge-robustness gap 1.59 (second smallest of nine models), and states that Qwen3-4B substantially outperforms Qwen3-30B, so robustness is not monotone in parameter count. arXiv:2601.18334 puts Qwen3-4B-Instruct at adjusted sycophancy 0.19 on MedQA under an expert nudge. A ~80-point discrepancy has to be absorbed by something, and "it is only a 4B" is closed.

**Answer available from the study's own data, partially.** Two of the three differences are structural and can be stated on the slide: (i) MED-STRESS conditions on turn-0-correct items by design, whereas C2 pools 350 correct-before, 24 incorrect-before and 26 indeterminate runs, the study already has that partition from C8 and can re-run C2 and C6 restricted to the 350 correct-before runs from existing logs, which makes the numbers commensurable; (ii) MED-STRESS runs multiple-choice medical QA with a fixed key, this study runs open selection from a formulary whose effective support is 3. **Concession required on the third:** this study's counts come from single greedy trajectories at temperature 0 with a fixed seed, so there is no run-to-run variance estimate at all, and 400/400 cannot be defended as a distributional statement. Say the reconciliation on the slide rather than waiting for the question.

### Attack 2. C4 and C5 are C2 and C3 restated. And there is no single-agent arm.

If the opening is constant, the opening is always abandoned, and the counterpart's drug is always adopted, then a perfectly diagonal cross-tab and a nonzero order-flip rate both follow arithmetically. Kim et al. (DOI 10.1038/s42256-026-01268-y) held prompts, tools and compute budgets constant across 260 configurations precisely so that this comparison would be interpretable; arXiv:2601.12307 and arXiv:2606.13003 make the compute-matched single agent the required control.

**Answer available from own data.** Compute, from existing transcripts, the fraction of the 82 order-flips predicted by the rule "final answer = the drug the counterpart named", and report the residual. Report chance-corrected agreement (kappa, or the expected diagonal rate under independence given the observed marginals) for C4, since agreement on a 3-category space with most mass on one category has a high chance floor. Report the directional split of the 82 flips, arXiv:2602.06526 uses near-symmetry (8 versus 14) as its evidence against first-speaker following, so a one-directional split is itself informative. **Concession required:** no compute-matched single-agent arm was run, so no claim of the form "multi-agent equals single-agent" can be made. The 175/200 in C1 is the opening turn of the debate pipeline, not a baseline.

### Attack 3. C8's sign is forced by base rates, so the asymmetry is not a behavioural finding.

arXiv:2604.18245 gives the break-even boundary c > [p0/(1-p0)] gamma. At p0 = 350/374 (determinate-only subset; the canonical partition is 341/22/37 of 400), the odds factor is 350/24 = 14.58; with gamma = 0.1486, break-even needs c > 2.17, which is unattainable. There are only 24 recoverable runs against 52 corrupted ones, so the step loses accuracy even at a 100% correction rate; any corruption rate above 24/350 = 6.9% forces net loss. Per unit, the model is better at correcting (54.2%) than at corrupting (14.9%), the same direction SycEval reports, progressive exceeding regressive; their rates are on a different base and are not comparable to ours.

**Concession, and it costs nothing to make.** Report the break-even arithmetic explicitly and lead with the quantity that is not forced: adequacy falls from 93.6% to 83.2%, a 10.4-point drop under the debate step, on an external clinical arbiter. Add Wilson intervals (52/350 is 11.5-19.0%; 13/24 is 35.1-72.1% and does not exclude a coin flip) and cluster at the case level, since C4 establishes the second agent adds no independent information, so the effective n is at most 200.

### Attack 4. You cannot attribute any of this to the counterpart clinician without a speaker-stripped arm.

arXiv:2607.05545 measures all four quantities: plain re-ask 10.3%, length-matched content-free control 19.7%, no-source floor (the assertion with the speaker deleted) 66.5%, expert-panel framing 79.4%, so the source-attributed increment is +12.9pp and roughly 67 of 79 points survive with the speaker removed. Worse for the peer-deference framing, minimal person labels score 57.4%, at or below the speaker-free floor, while a "retrieved reference" container scores 80.4%: persuasion tracks evidential cast, not the presence of a clinician. Their stated thesis is that peer-pressure benchmarks without a speaker-free control conflate social influence with susceptibility to repeated assertion. Separately, arXiv:2606.00820 shows a naive stance-only flip rate of ~47% falling to 29.30% once self-reflection stability is required as a counterfactual, so roughly 40% of apparent peer-induced change is spontaneous instability.

**Partial answer from own data, then a concession.** The neutral re-ask arm at 0/200 does pre-empt the spontaneous-instability confound in the specific sense that this checkpoint shows no self-reflection drift at temperature 0, that is a genuine methodological advantage over arXiv:2606.00820 and should be stated. ### Attack 5. The arbiter barely discriminates on this cohort, and its optimum is a stewardship error.

Constant meropenem covers 192/200 = 96.0%, so the entire discriminable band is 8.5 points, 17 cases. Liu et al. (rs-9854692) independently report meropenem-for-all at 93.2% (179/192) on MIMIC-IV gram-negative BSI and note limited decision-curve interpretability for meropenem because susceptible prevalence was high. ## 4. What the literature says that this project should adopt

**Competence-conditioning, before anything else.** MED-STRESS (arXiv:2605.23932) retains only turn-0-correct instances; arXiv:2608.01017 retains only trials correct before the challenge; arXiv:2607.05545 defines harmful revision as Pr(a1 != t given a0 = t); arXiv:2606.00820 conditions on self-reflection stability. Re-run C2 and C6 restricted to the 350 correct-before runs. This is a re-analysis of logs already on disk and it is what makes the numbers comparable to every rate a referee will hold them against.

**The speaker-stripped arm and the invalid-label placebo** (arXiv:2607.05545). Deleting the persona from the challenge text gives the no-source floor; asserting a drug not in the formulary gives the placebo (their floor collapses 66.5% to 15.2%), which directly tests whether revision requires a valid landing option. With a 3-drug effective answer space, that test is load-bearing here.

**The form-versus-content rung** (arXiv:2606.00820). A counterpart turn that is reasoning-shaped but vacuous, versus one that is substantively wrong, separates persuasion by argument shape from persuasion by clinical content: 29.66% versus 54.48% in their data, with form contributing 39-72%. Without it, "a reasoned counterpart turn moved the model" cannot be attributed to the reasoning.

**A paired noise-floor protocol** (arXiv:2606.20695). Report seed-paired and order-paired gaps with intervals before any coordination or order claim; their finding that seven of ten recent coordination architectures report effects below the local noise floor is the framing this project should adopt rather than resist.

**A No-Interaction control** (arXiv:2603.28813). Protocol effects and model effects are otherwise inseparable, and the perfectly diagonal cross-tab cannot be attributed to the agents rather than to the termination rule.

**A compute-matched single-agent arm** (Kim et al., DOI 10.1038/s42256-026-01268-y; arXiv:2601.12307; arXiv:2606.13003 uses CoT-SC as the cost-matched comparator; arXiv:2605.00914 prices debate at 2.1-3.4x tokens). Self-consistency over roughly five samples of the same checkpoint on the same 200 cases. Without it C4 has no baseline.

**Within-checkpoint prompt variation on the opening turn, plus one full-precision and one temperature>0 arm.** arXiv:2608.03854 (prompt template moves biomedical accuracy 7-24pp, rivalling quantisation), arXiv:2505.18949 (format-induced diversity collapse persists at high temperature), arXiv:2604.19884 (the quantisation cliff is at 2-bit, which defends the 4-bit choice), arXiv:2608.02827 (temperature is the noise term; low temperature amplifies initial bias into consensus). These four together turn C11 from an assertion into a controlled claim.

**Chance-corrected agreement and directional splits.** Kappa for the C4 cross-tab; the 8-versus-14 style directional split for C5's 82 flips, following arXiv:2602.06526.

**The two-rate vocabulary and the break-even test** (arXiv:2604.18245). Report c and gamma under their names, then evaluate the break-even boundary at the observed baseline. It converts C8 from a contestable asymmetry claim into an audited instance.

**Wilson intervals and case-level clustering throughout.** Every headline integer in this study is a point estimate from one greedy trajectory; arXiv:2608.01017 finds question-level variance exceeding model-level variance 67-fold across 1.2M trials, so item sampling dominates and a 200-case single-decode estimate needs its interval printed next to it.

**An annotation protocol with inter-rater reliability for C10.** Hazare et al. (PMID 42238440) validated against physician raters at Cohen's kappa 0.957; the npj 2026 agent study (DOI 10.1038/s41746-026-02443-6) uses an explicit two-category taxonomy with per-scenario counts; the 2025 summarisation framework (DOI 10.1038/s41746-025-01670-7) used 12,999 clinician-annotated sentences. Add the fact-controlled Leave-N-out ablation (arXiv:2506.00448) if the leakage question is to be answered rather than gestured at.

**A stewardship endpoint alongside coverage.** Antonie et al. make guardrail violations primary and in-vitro activity secondary; Corbin et al. impose spectrum constraints; Pato et al. argue the general case. Reporting spectrum breadth next to adequacy is what stops the constant-meropenem comparison reading as a recommendation.

**A heterogeneous or mixed-vendor arm, if time allows.** arXiv:2603.04421 reports mixed-vendor teams outperforming single-vendor by pooling complementary inductive biases; arXiv:2606.19826 shows harmful revision falling from 89% to 35% when the peer is an honest heterogeneous model. Homogeneous debate is the worst-case configuration in that literature, which bounds what C4 can claim.

**Denominator transparency and a collider-bias sentence.** Antonie's 32.0% and Liu's 19.1% microbiology-evaluability rates set the expectation this study's 100% has to meet.
