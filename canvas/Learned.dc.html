What the summer actually taught me

The measurement is the contribution

Read before you build

Agents conferring is already known not to reliably help. Sycophancy is already documented. Knowing that is what stopped me claiming either.

The control is the cheap part

The most useful thing I built was the condition where somebody speaks and says nothing. It cost almost nothing and everything else rests on it.

Pick the endpoint honestly

Score only whether the answer was right and this failure is invisible. The endpoint you choose decides what you are able to see.

If you evaluate a multi-agent clinical system on correctness alone, you will not see this.
