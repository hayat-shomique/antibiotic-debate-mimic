What I got wrong

Three times the data flattered me and I had to go back

01
A safety check was deleting my data

It threw away any run where the model itself wrote the word “resistant”, which removed 220 runs and removed exactly the ones where it was reasoning about microbiology. One arm then read a perfect 160 out of 160. The perfection was the clue.

02
My best-looking result was a confound

A score of 2.18 looked like the model reading evidence. I held the drug fixed and varied only the patient, and it was exactly zero.

03
I set a test that could not fail

I pre-registered “confident” at 80 out of 100, and every single answer came back 85, 90 or 95. A threshold nothing can fall below is not a test.
